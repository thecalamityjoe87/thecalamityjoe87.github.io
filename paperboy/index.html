Paperboy

A beautiful native news reader for the Linux desktop, built with GTK4 and Libadwaita. Curated sources, custom feeds, live sports, podcasts, and local news — all in one clean, distraction-free app.

Download Paperboy Read the README

Features

Everything you need to stay informed

Paperboy pulls your news together in one native, GNOME-friendly app — no browser tabs required.

  • 📰

    Curated sources

    Ready to go out of the box with The Guardian, BBC, FOX News, NPR, PBS, ABC News, Bloomberg, the NYT, and the WSJ.

  • ⚡

    Powered by PaperboyAPI

    Fetches articles from multiple sources and categories seamlessly, in the background.

  • ⭐

    Follow any source

    Discover and follow additional sources you find through the API as you browse.

  • 📡

    RSS feed support

    Add any RSS feed to follow your favorite websites and blogs alongside the news.

  • 🛠️

    Customizable feeds

    Mix and match sources and categories to build a news stream that's entirely yours.

  • 📖

    In-app reading

    Read full articles without leaving the app, with a built-in ad blocker for a cleaner experience.

  • 🌍

    Local news

    Uses your location, via GeoClue, to surface news for your area automatically.

  • 🏆

    Live sports scores

    Track live and recent game scores from a dedicated sports view.

  • 🎙️

    Podcasts

    Discover and subscribe to podcasts, with built-in playback, a persistent mini-player, and played/new episode tracking.

  • 🔗

    Share articles

    Quickly share the articles you find to other apps.

Note: Paperboy is in active development (alpha). Expect changes and occasional breakage.
